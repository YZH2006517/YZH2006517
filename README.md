<h3 align="center">让照片，慢慢发光。</h3>

<p align="center">一张可以开灯、摆片、放大细节的电子观片台，也是一套从选片到修图出片的本地工作台。<br><b>免费开源 · 本地处理 · 原片只读</b></p>

| 按键 | 观片台 | |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | **自由摆片** | 拖动、逐张缩放、一键平铺。台面怎么摆，下次回来还是那样。 |
| <kbd>V</kbd> | **拿起放大镜** | 在鼠标位置开镜，倍率和镜片尺寸分开调，镜外还看得见整张台面。 |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | **连续胶卷** | 首尾相接，横向滑动。可以自动播放，手一碰就停。 |
| <kbd>I</kbd> | **只剩照片** | 按空格或碰到屏幕边缘才唤出工具，片孔、边框装饰都能关。 |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | **关灯** | 关灯后照片只透一点光，底色有米黄、暖灰、灰黑，也能自定义。 |
| ⌂ | **胶片收藏室** | 图集分卷收上三维胶卷架，每卷 36 帧。选一卷，暗盒落桌、胶片展开。 |

<p align="center">Most of my care goes into <b>Photo All</b>: a light table on Windows, a film camera on Android.<br>Both are free, open source, and keep your photos on your own device.</p>

### [Photo All Studio](https://github.com/YZH2006517/Photo-All) — let your photos slowly glow

*Et lux in tenebris lucet* — And the light shineth in darkness (John 1:5)

A light table you can switch on, lay photos across and magnify — and a local workbench that goes from picking to grading to the final print. **Free & open source · processed locally · originals read-only.**

| Key | The light table | |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | **Lay them out freely** | Drag, resize one by one, tile in one key. The table stays the way you left it. |
| <kbd>V</kbd> | **Pick up the loupe** | A refracting lens opens where the cursor is; the rest of the table stays in view. |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | **A continuous roll** | Frames join end to end and slide sideways; it plays itself and pauses when touched. |
| <kbd>I</kbd> | **Nothing but the photos** | Tools appear only on Space or at the screen edge. |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | **Lights off** | The photos let only a little light through — beige, warm grey or charcoal beneath. |
| ⌂ | **The film archive** | Albums become rolls of 36 frames on a 3D shelf; pick one and it unrolls on the desk. |

**126** open LUTs · **17** creative looks · **16** Spektrafilm stocks · **16** stackable layers. Expose first, then print: film → develop → enlarger → paper, not a colour filter laid on top. Borders in 35mm, 120, rough scan, slide mount or paper margin, with your own roll name, date, frame number and signature.

[**Free download**](https://github.com/YZH2006517/Photo-All/releases) · [Source](https://github.com/YZH2006517/Photo-All) — Windows 10/11 x64, GPL-3.0

### [Photo All · Film Camera](https://github.com/YZH2006517/Photo-All-Android) — press the shutter, the darkroom does the rest

*Omnia tempus habent* — To every thing there is a season (Ecclesiastes 3:1)

The viewfinder already shows the film. After the shot, the photo (RAW by default) is developed on the phone's GPU — exposed to film, developed, printed to paper — in about three seconds. **22** film stocks · **9** papers · **416** creative LUTs.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The viewfinder is the print** | Live film preview that matches the final brightness and colour. |
| **The shutter becomes a dial** | Circle the shutter to set LUT strength — clockwise richer, anticlockwise lighter. |
| **Flash that holds back** | One ambient frame plus one flash frame, re-balanced in RAW to darken the background. |
| **Turn it sideways** | A glowing fan appears; tap it for a layout with a scroll wheel, dial and lens ring. |
| **Five themes** | Film canister, darkroom red light, paper, brass, cold silver. |
| **Every frame remembered** | Camera, exposure, focus and every decision the engine made, one tap to copy. |
